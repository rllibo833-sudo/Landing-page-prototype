# ECONOMIC PATH — PUBLIC

The digital foundation is not being built only as a showcase.

The immediate practical test is whether the system can help produce a small, useful, reviewable deliverable for a **real external need**.

## First paid pilot

**AI-assisted Operations / Research Pack**

For a concrete operational or research problem, the deliverable can include:

- decision brief;
- evidence/source register;
- assumptions and reasoning;
- recommended workflow;
- implementation checklist;
- limitations and unresolved questions.

### Regulatory/legal research boundary

For regulation-related requests, the pack may include:

- relevant regulations from official sources;
- source/provenance register;
- evidence extracted from those sources;
- questions to bring to a qualified legal professional;
- documentation and verification checklist;
- unresolved items marked **UNVERIFIED / NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW**.

It does **not** provide legal opinions, legal determinations, compliance guarantees, or substitute for qualified legal counsel.

The same boundary applies to medical, tax, investment, and other regulated/high-stakes matters.

## Real-use requirement

A request must be concrete enough to define:

- the problem/question;
- intended use or decision;
- requested output;
- acceptance criteria;
- relevant constraints.

Incomplete requests are not converted into guessed answers.

## Engagement path

**REQUEST → QUALIFY → FOUNDER REVIEW → SCOPE & PRICE → DELIVER → ACCEPT → PAY → PROVE**

The Founder does not promise acceptance, turnaround, or outcome before reviewing the request.

## Evidence standard

Material claims must be traceable to their source. Conflicting evidence, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved questions must remain visible.

**No Proof, No Claim.**
