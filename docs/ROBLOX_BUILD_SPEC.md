# 3D / Roblox Visualization — Kawasan Masjid 1.000 Ha

The 1,000 Ha masterplan is the long-term physical destination. The Roblox specification is a visualization/build layer derived from the Founder-provided masterplan.

## What visitors should understand

The 3D representation should make the masterplan readable from:

- aerial / bird's-eye view;
- ground-level exploration;
- the central mosque and ceremonial axis;
- the relationship between worship, education, economy, housing, agriculture, ecology and mobility.

The visualization should feel spatial and believable, not like an abstract decorative 3D scene.

## Shared spatial anchors

- Grand Mosque at the center.
- Main Plaza south of the mosque.
- Main Gate south on the central axis.
- Education north.
- Lake/recreation west.
- Water-management reservoirs northwest.
- Agriculture and livestock north/northeast.
- Community housing southwest.
- Market, dormitories and staff housing east/southeast.
- Plantation and utilities southeast.
- Parking and outer ring road around the mobility perimeter.

## Visualization scale

The supplied Roblox build specification recommends Scale B / 1:20, approximately 600 studs across the site.

The visualization should preserve the masterplan relationships even when UI, camera, rendering or interaction is adapted for the web.

## Public truth boundary

This is a **future visualization** of the masterplan.

PUBLIC must not imply that the physical 1,000 Ha kawasan has already been built. Digital prototypes, Roblox scenes, 3D renders, maps or animations are presentation/prototyping artifacts, not construction proof.
