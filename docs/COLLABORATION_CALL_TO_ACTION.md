# Public Collaboration Call to Action

**Status:** Content guidance; this file does not confirm that a live form or partner network exists.

## Purpose

Help interested people understand the initiative, review available evidence, and propose a specific contribution.

## Recommended public message

### Help shape the next verifiable step

We are developing an open, evidence-led initiative around the kawasan-masjid concept. The software, governance, and public information are still being built, and not every proposed facility, funding source, permit, or physical activity is confirmed.

We welcome conversations with people who can contribute practical expertise, research, software, data/GIS, community insight, or a carefully scoped pilot. Please review the current project information and share what you could contribute, what conditions you need, and what evidence would help you assess the proposal.

Submitting interest is an introduction, not a partnership agreement or a promise of funding or participation. Any collaboration will be confirmed explicitly and described publicly only with permission and verifiable evidence.

## Required link destinations before publishing

- Current project overview and truthful status.
- Public source repository / roadmap.
- A working, privacy-aware interest submission channel.
- A contact method that the Founder actively monitors.
- Privacy notice and consent language.

If any destination is not working, do not display a button that implies it is operational. Do not invent testimonials, partner logos, institutional endorsements, impact figures, or funding status.

## Public reporting

Report enquiries, conversations, written agreements, and completed work separately. Publish names, affiliations, or quotations only with consent and accurate attribution.
